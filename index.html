Splash Auto Detail is a fixed-location detail shop in Chula Vista's Eastlake district, open since 2011. The shop provides expert car wash and detailing for every kind of vehicle, from daily commuters to luxury automobiles, with interior and exterior detail packages at a dedicated facility. Fifteen years in, Splash has built a loyal South Bay customer base serving sedans, trucks, SUVs, luxury cars, and EVs alike.
